# Datastore Setup

### Choice of Datastore

For this assignment, I chose MongoDB as the datastore. MongoDB is a NoSQL database known for its flexibility and scalability. Here’s why MongoDB was selected. I have been a little
more familiar with the SQL options, so I wanted to try one I was not as familiar with out!
-While looking into MongoDB, I realized that it develops quick and is easier to scale or adjust when compared to the other options. This made it a good fit for this assignment.
